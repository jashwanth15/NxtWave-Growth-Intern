## Slide 1: Understand the Student – The 2025 Placement Dilemma

### 1. Target Audience Persona
- **Target:** Final-Year B.Tech/B.E. students (2025/2026 Batch) in Tier-2, Tier-3, and Tier-4 colleges across India.
- **Segments:**
  - *Circuit Branches (CSE/IT/ECE):* Facing campus placement hiring slowdowns and ATS resume auto-rejections.
  - *Non-Circuit Branches (Mech/Civil/EEE):* Aspiring to pivot to software and tech roles (NxtWave CCBP core audience), but paralyzed by code complexity.

### 2. Why Would They Care? (The Psychological Pain Point)
- **The "Cloned Project" Rejection Crisis:** 90% of students have identical resume projects (Basic To-Do app, Netflix UI clone, Weather widget, or standard Kaggle Iris dataset).
- **Recruiter Shift in 2025:** Tech recruiters and automated ATS filters now prioritize candidates with practical **GenAI & LLM API integration experience**.
- **Impending Urgency:** 8th-semester major project submission deadlines and final campus placement drives are happening right now.

### 3. What Makes Them Register? (The High-Value Offer)
- **Not a passive webinar:** Students have webinar fatigue.
- **The 60-Minute Verifiable Tangible Outcome:**
  1. A **live deployed GenAI web application** with a shareable URL to put on their resume and LinkedIn.
  2. A **GitHub repository** with clean documentation to prove technical ownership.
  3. **Verified ATS-optimized resume bullet points** tailored for entry-level tech roles.

> **Core Value Proposition:** *"Don't just add Python on your resume. Build & deploy a live GenAI web app in 60 minutes to beat the 2025 ATS placement filter."*

## Slide 2: Channel Prioritization & ₹2,000 Budget Unit Economics

### Why Paid Ads Are Mathematically Flawed
- Target: 500 registrations with ₹2,000 budget = **Max allowable CAC of ₹4.00**.
- In India, Meta/Instagram ad CPMs for tech audiences range from ₹140 to ₹200.
- With an average CTR of 1.5% and a landing page conversion rate of 15%, ₹2,000 generates at most **25–30 registrations (CAC ~₹75)**.

## Slide 4: The Working Asset – AI Placement Diagnostic & Viral Ticket Engine

Everyone builds a generic landing page with a countdown timer. We engineered a full Product-Led Growth (PLG) web application:

1. **The Hook: Interactive Resume Diagnostic Grader**
   - Students benchmark their resume against 2025 hiring standards in 45 seconds.
   - Calculates their ATS Deficit Score (e.g. 32/100) and highlights the lack of deployed GenAI projects as the root vulnerability.
   - Converts diagnostic anxiety into an immediate registration intent.

2. **Personalized VIP Boarding Pass Generator**
   - Dynamic Canvas image generation (`.png` download) with unique ticket ID (`#NW-AI-8492`), date, time, and verified QR check-in badge.

3. **Product-Led Viral Referral Gate (The 3-Peer Loop)**

   - Features 1-click WhatsApp sharing with pre-formatted, student-to-student copy.
   - Evaluators can test the referral progression live via the simulation button!

4. **Internal Growth Ops Command Center**
   - Evaluator toggle displaying live CAC, channel attribution, conversion percentages, and registration velocity.

## Slide 5: Critical Thinking, AI Judgment & Learning Notes

### What Changed Between First Idea and Final Solution?
- **First Idea:** Spend ₹2,000 on Instagram reels targeting 20-22 year olds; send traffic to a standard registration page with speaker bios.
- **Why It Was Scrapped:** Realized the unit economics were mathematically impossible (CAC ~₹75). Replaced passive registration with an **interactive diagnostic tool** and reallocated 100% of the budget into **CR micro-bounties and viral unlock mechanics**.

### What Did AI Suggest That Was Deliberately Rejected?
1. **AI Suggested Email Drip Sequences:** Engineering students in Tier-2/3 India check personal emails infrequently (<12% open rate). Deliberately rejected in favor of a **WhatsApp-first engagement loop (92%+ open rate)**.
2. **AI Suggested a 7-Field Registration Form:** AI wanted to collect CGPA, backlogs, LinkedIn URL, and full address. Deliberately rejected to prevent 70%+ drop-off, simplifying to **Name + WhatsApp number**.

### If Given Another 24 Hours, What Would We Improve?
1. **WhatsApp Cloud API Webhook:** Automatically deliver the personalized ticket and automated reminder alerts via WhatsApp sandbox.
2. **TPO (Training & Placement Officer) Institutional 1-Pager:** A zero-cost institutional deck allowing college placement officers to broadcast the workshop to core-branch students seeking IT placement.
