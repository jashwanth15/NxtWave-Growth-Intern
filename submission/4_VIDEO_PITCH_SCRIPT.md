# Deliverable 4: 3-Minute Video Pitch Script & Walkthrough

**Format:** Screen Recording + Webcam Bubble (e.g. via Loom or OBS)  
**Total Target Duration:** 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Objective:** Present your growth strategy, demonstrate the live working asset, explain your unit economics, and highlight your critical thinking and deliberate rejection of AI suggestions.

---

## Video Production & Delivery Tips
1. **Tooling:** Use [Loom](https://www.loom.com) (free) or OBS Studio. Keep your face webcam visible in the corner with your screen sharing `PRESENTATION_DECK.html` and `asset/index.html`.
2. **Tone:** Energetic, confident, analytical, and data-backed. Speak like an ownership-driven Growth Lead who understands student psychology and unit economics.
3. **Pacing:** ~130–140 words per minute. Practice 2–3 times before recording the final take.

---

## Timed Word-for-Word Script

### [0:00 – 0:35] Part 1: The Hook, Persona & Psychological Pain Point
**Screen View:** Display **Slide 1** of `PRESENTATION_DECK.html` or the Diagnostic hero screen.

> *"Hi NxtWave team! When tackling the challenge of getting 500 final-year engineering students to register in just 7 days with a ₹2,000 budget, my first realization was that standard webinar marketing would completely fail.*
>
> *Final-year students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges don't need another generic lecture. They are facing severe placement anxiety: tech hiring is down, and ATS algorithms are ruthlessly rejecting resumes with cloned projects like To-Do apps and Netflix clones.*
>
> *Our angle isn't 'attend a webinar'—it's: 'Don't just mention Python on your resume; build and deploy a live GenAI web application in 60 minutes to beat the 2025 ATS placement filter.' We give them 3 tangible proofs: a live URL, a GitHub repo, and ATS-verified resume bullet points."*

---

### [0:35 – 1:15] Part 2: Growth Strategy & Unit Economics (₹2,000 Budget)
**Screen View:** Switch to **Slide 2** of `PRESENTATION_DECK.html` (Unit Economics & Funnel).

> *"Now, let's talk unit economics. 500 registrations on a ₹2,000 budget means our maximum allowable CAC is ₹4.00 per student.*
>
> *When I asked AI for ideas, it immediately suggested running Instagram and Meta ads. I ran the math and rejected it immediately: at ₹180 CPM and a 15% conversion rate, ₹2,000 would buy only 27 registrations at ₹74 CAC—wasting 95% of our budget.*
>
> *Instead, I deployed 100% of the ₹2,000 into a high-leverage Class Representative (CR) Bounty Engine. By offering ₹800, ₹500, ₹400, and ₹300 vouchers to top college CRs, we tap into official, locked WhatsApp groups with 100% reach and institutional trust.*
>
> *Paired with a viral 3-peer student referral gate, this generates 250 registrations from CRs, 175 from viral peer loops, and 75 from placement hubs, achieving 518 registrations at a blended CAC of just ₹3.86."*

---

### [1:15 – 2:15] Part 3: Live Demo of the Working Asset
**Screen View:** Switch to `asset/index.html` in browser. Interact with the tool live.

> *"Instead of building a passive landing page that everyone builds, I engineered a Product-Led Growth asset: the AI Placement Diagnostic & Viral Ticket Engine.*
>
> *[Action: Click through Step 1 and Step 2 of Diagnostic]*  
> *When a student visits, they don't see a boring form. They benchmark their resume in 45 seconds. By selecting their branch and current projects, the tool calculates their ATS AI Deficit Score—here showing 32 out of 100—highlighting that their lack of deployed GenAI projects is putting them at risk.*
>
> *[Action: Enter details and click 'Generate My VIP Ticket']*  
> *The workshop is positioned as the instant 60-minute cure. With just their name and WhatsApp number, we generate a personalized VIP Boarding Pass using HTML5 Canvas—complete with their name, college, ticket number, and QR code, downloadable with one click as a PNG image.*
>
> *[Action: Click 'Simulate Peer Registration' 3 times to unlock vault]*  
> *Notice our viral referral loop: students can unlock the VIP AI Project Starter Vault—including the GitHub repository and 50 ATS resume bullets—by inviting 3 classmates. As classmates register, the progress bar fills up live and unlocks the vault with instant celebration feedback.*
>
> *[Action: Click 'Growth Analytics (INTERNAL)' in header]*  
> *I also built an internal Growth Ops dashboard where the NxtWave team can monitor real-time visitor-to-pass conversion, channel attribution, and 7-day registration velocity."*

---

### [2:15 – 3:00] Part 4: Critical Thinking, AI Rejections & Learnings
**Screen View:** Switch to **Slide 5** of `PRESENTATION_DECK.html`.

> *"Throughout this build, AI was an incredible velocity booster, but I had to actively overrule it on strategic decisions:*
>
> *First, AI pushed for a 5-step email drip campaign. I rejected this because Indian engineering students rarely check emails; instead, I built a WhatsApp-first loop with 92% open rates and automated .ics calendar downloads to guarantee workshop attendance.*
>
> *Second, AI suggested a 7-field form asking for CGPA and college history, which would have killed conversion. I stripped it down to just Name and Phone number.*
>
> *If I had another 24 hours, I'd integrate the WhatsApp Cloud API webhook for direct ticket delivery into their chat and create a zero-cost institutional 1-pager for College Placement Officers.*
>
> *I am extremely excited about the Growth Intern role at NxtWave because I love combining growth hacking, deep student psychology, and a relentless bias to ship. Thank you!"*
