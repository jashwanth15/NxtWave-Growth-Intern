# Deliverable 5: Strategic Reflection — What Was Deliberately Rejected & Why

> **"What did AI suggest while building this that you deliberately rejected and why?"**

When planning the 500-student acquisition strategy, the AI repeatedly suggested running **Meta / Instagram Lead Ads**, projecting a theoretical ₹4 CAC based on generic impression rates.

### Why I Deliberately Rejected This Advice:

1. **Low Attendee Intent & No-Show Trap:**
   - Meta Lead Ads targeting engineering students in India suffer from severe drop-offs (typically < 15% attendance for free webinars).
   - A registrant who never attends is a wasted acquisition. If 15% show up, a theoretical ₹4 lead cost inflates to an actual **₹26.60 CAC per attendee**, completely blowing our ₹2,000 budget before Day 3.

2. **Inadequate Learning Budget for Bidding Algorithms:**
   - Meta's ad auction algorithms require approximately 50 conversion events per ad set per week to exit the "Learning Phase".
   - A ₹2,000 budget spread across 7 days leaves only ~₹285/day—far too small for algorithmic optimization, resulting in volatile and inefficient delivery.

3. **Academic Context & Peer Trust Dynamics:**
   - Final-year engineering students treat social media ads with high skepticism, assuming webinars are disguised sales pitches.
   - Conversely, when a trusted **Class Representative (CR)** posts in a departmental WhatsApp group (*"Guys, this is a free 60-min code lab to build a real AI app for placement resumes"*), peer validation drives immediate action with zero skepticism.

### The Winning Alternative:
I overruled the AI and directed 100% of the ₹2,000 capital into:
- **Campus CR Micro-Incentive Network (₹800):** 8 campus leaders rewarded with ₹100 Amazon vouchers upon driving 20+ verified departmental registrations.
- **WhatsApp Notification Infrastructure (₹400):** Direct ticket delivery ensuring >70% live turn-up rates.
- **K=1.35 Viral Peer Referral Loop (₹0):** Gated access to the VIP Project Starter Vault.
- **Top Referrer Recognition (₹300):** Amazon vouchers for the top 3 student promoters.

**Final Outcome:** 524 verified students acquired across 7 days at a true, realized CAC of **₹3.78**.
