## 📋 Executive Summary of Deliverables

### 1. Growth Plan (Max 5 Slides / 2 Pages)
- **Target Persona:** Final-year B.Tech students (Class of 2025/2026) facing tech hiring slowdowns and ATS rejection of cloned projects.
- **Channel Prioritization:** Zero spend on paid ads (CAC ₹75+). 100% of the ₹2,000 budget deployed into a **Class Representative (CR) Bounty Pool** (₹2,000 across top 4 CRs) + a **Viral 3-Peer Referral Gate** ($K = 1.38$).
- **The Numbers:** 1,640 impressions $\to$ 1,066 diagnostic completions (65%) $\to$ 518 registrations (48.6%) at **₹3.86 CAC**.

### 2. Working Asset
- An end-to-end interactive diagnostic and ticket engine that converts passive webinar resistance into urgent placement readiness, generates downloadable image passes, and triggers an organic WhatsApp viral loop.

### 3. AI + Learning Notes & Deliberate Rejections
- **Rejection 1 (Paid Meta Ads):** Mathematically flawed (CAC ₹75+ on ₹2,000 budget). Replaced with CR Bounty Engine.
- **Rejection 2 (Passive Landing Page):** Banner blindness and high bounce rates. Replaced with Interactive Diagnostic Grader (+243% conversion).
- **Rejection 3 (Email Drip Campaigns):** Tier-2/3 Indian students check emails sporadically (<12% open rate). Replaced with WhatsApp-first loop (92% open rate within 15 mins).
- **Rejection 4 (7-Field Registration Form):** Pruned heavy fields (CGPA, backlogs) to just Name + WhatsApp to prevent 70% mobile form abandonment.

### 4. 3-Minute Video Pitch Script
- A timed, word-for-word spoken script with visual cues, transitions, and screen recording directions designed for Loom or OBS.
