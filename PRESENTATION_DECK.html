Critical Thinking, Learning & AI Rejections

High Ownership & Judgment

Q1. What Changed Between 1st Idea & Final Solution?

First Idea: A typical modern landing page with a speaker bio, syllabus list, countdown timer, and ₹2,000 spent on Instagram reels.

Final Solution: Realized paid ads yield ~27 leads at ₹74 CAC. Replaced landing page with an AI Placement Diagnostic + 3-Peer Viral WhatsApp Gate and put the ₹2,000 into a CR Bounty Leaderboard.

Q2. What Deliberately Rejected from AI & Why?

AI Suggested: "Send a 5-step email nurture drip sequence to students and use a 7-field registration form."

Deliberately Rejected: Engineering students in India have <12% email open rates. Replaced completely with a WhatsApp-First Loop (92% open rate) and pruned the form to just 2 fields (Name + Phone) to eliminate 70% form abandonment.
